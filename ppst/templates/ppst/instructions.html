Philadelphia Pointing Span Test - Instructions

How This Test Works

You will be presented with sequences of numbers and letters. Your task is to remember them and then click the symbols in the exact order they were shown.

What to Expect:

  1. Watch and Listen: A sequence of numbers or letters will appear on screen one at a time and be read aloud to you.
  2. Remember: Pay close attention and remember the sequence you see and hear.
  3. Respond: After the sequence is presented, click the symbols in the exact order you saw them.
  4. Feedback: During practice, you will receive feedback on your responses.

Example 1:

Sequence shown: 5 - 2 - 8

Your response: Click 5, then 2, then 8

Example 2:

Sequence shown: 5 - A - 2 - C - 8 - B

Your response: Click 5, then A, then 2, then C, then 8, then B

Test Information: You will complete 12 test trials. These trials will be scored. Take your time and do your best.
